Our mission

When money delays care, OCDyssey helps people move forward.

OCD and hoarding can deeply affect daily life, relationships, health, and safety. OCDyssey raises funds so people who do not have the means can still access therapy or hoarding cleanup support.

The goal is simple: make help more reachable, more humane, and more immediate for the people who need it.

How we help

Support that feels practical, hopeful, and human.

OCDyssey is designed around the needs people often face when they are ready for help but cannot fully afford it.

01

Therapy assistance

Help cover the costs that make therapy feel out of reach, from getting started to staying engaged in care.

  • Intake and early-session support
  • Short-term scholarship help
  • Practical barriers that delay treatment
02

Hoarding cleanup support

Fund respectful, safety-focused cleanup help that centers dignity and reduces overwhelm for households in need.

  • Cleanup supplies and labor support
  • Safer living-space planning
  • Compassion over shame
03

Connection and follow-through

Make it easier for people, families, and partners to reach out and stay connected to the right next step.

  • Support request pathways
  • Loved-one and partner outreach
  • Community-minded coordination

FAQ

Helpful answers for donors, families, and partners.

This website is designed to make the mission clear and give people an easy next step.

What does OCDyssey fund?

OCDyssey fundraises so people with OCD who do not have the funds can access therapy and hoarding cleanup support.

How do donations work on this site?

The donation button takes visitors to OCDyssey's Zeffy page, where giving is completed securely.

Can families, clinicians, or community partners reach out too?

Yes. The site includes pathways for individuals seeking help, loved ones, and professional or community partners.

How can I contact OCDyssey directly?
